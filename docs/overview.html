prefers-*

Five small questions your browser can ask about your visitors, and what to do with the answers.

Each card shows your own browser's answer, live, plus the pain point behind the query and an honest support note. The full demo and pattern for each one lives on its own site.

prefers-reduced-data

Your page costs real money on a metered plan.

Your browser says…

Support: limited. Chromium exposes it behind a flag; Firefox and Safari do not implement it. The Save-Data request header reaches further.

prefers-contrast

Your quiet grey text simply disappears for someone with low vision.

Your browser says…

Support: Baseline since late 2024. Chrome and Edge 96, Firefox 101, Safari 14.1 on macOS and 14.5 on iOS.

prefers-reduced-transparency
